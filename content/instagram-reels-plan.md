# Zenix Instagram Reels Plan: 20-Reel Series with the CEO Presenter

Builds on the research doc dated 20 Sep 2026 (Videos 1 to 10). Status: draft for owner approval. Nothing here is published or generated yet.

## 1. Recommendation

Produce **20 reels in 4 batches of 5**, but change three things from the original research:

| # | Change | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Flip the mix from 60% broad / 20% supermarket to **45% supermarket-first, 30% broad marketing education, 10% AI automation, 15% CEO POV and proof** | Zenix's position is supermarket-first. Broad education grows followers, but the buyers we want (US supermarket owners, retainers of $500+ a month) only self-identify when the hook names their problem. Critical assumption in the old mix: broad reach converts into supermarket leads. Unproven. |
| 2 | Move scripts from 15s to **25 to 35s** (about 60 to 75 spoken words) | Instagram now counts total watch time including replays. A third-party study of about 140,000 business Reels found 45 to 60s clips had the highest median views, and 15 to 30s the best completion. 25 to 35s is the safe middle. Test one 45s reel in batch 3. |
| 3 | Cut **10 keywords down to 5** (PLAN, PAGE, POSTER, MAP, GROW), each tied to one deliverable | You cannot build and maintain 10 lead magnets before the first reel goes live. Five is deliverable with 3 to 5 new clients a month capacity. |

## 4. Batch 1: final scripts (ready for CEO approval)

Each is about 65 to 75 words. Confirm timing with the actual voice before generation.

**Reel 1. Boosting is not a strategy** (Setup A, DM PLAN)
"Boosting a post is not a strategy. It is a button. Before you spend anything, decide three things. The offer: what are you actually selling this week? The audience: who should see it? And the action: do you want a call, a visit, or a message? Build the creative around that one action. If you want the checklist we use, DM PLAN."
On-screen: BOOSTING IS A BUTTON | OFFER, AUDIENCE, ACTION | DM PLAN

**Reel 2. The crowded sale ad** (Setup B, cut to C for CTA, DM POSTER)
"Too many offers can hide your best offer. Look at your weekly supermarket poster. If a shopper cannot find the first deal in two seconds, they will scroll past all of them. Pick one hero deal and make it the biggest thing on the page. Then show two supporting specials and your store details. DM POSTER and we will review yours."
On-screen: ONE HERO DEAL | TWO SUPPORTING SPECIALS | STORE DETAILS | DM POSTER
Finishing: add the real poster mockup in editing, not in the video model.

**Reel 3. Can shoppers find your store** (Setup B, cut to C, DM MAP)
"Before you buy more ads, check whether nearby shoppers can find your store. Open your Google Business Profile. Is the address right? Are the hours current, including holidays? Is the category accurate? Are the photos recent? Google's own guidance says complete, accurate information helps local results, so fix the basics first. DM MAP for the checklist."
On-screen: ADDRESS | HOURS | CATEGORY | PHOTOS | DM MAP
Do not show rankings or promise visibility.

**Reel 4. More traffic will not fix it** (Setup A, DM PAGE)
"More traffic will not fix a confusing website. If your ads send people to a page that hides the offer, you are paying for people to leave. Check three things. Is the offer obvious? Is there proof? Is there one clear next step? Fix the page first, then buy more clicks. DM PAGE and I will send our checklist."
On-screen: OFFER | PROOF | NEXT STEP | DM PAGE
Finishing: composite a clean wireframe. Do not generate a readable website.

**Reel 5. What specific proof sounds like** (Setup D, DM GROW)
"Most agencies say they get results. Here is what specific proof sounds like. Khaamar Baari Supermarket achieved 400% revenue growth in one year while working with Zenix. Notice the structure: a named client, a clear result, and a timeframe. That is how you should judge any agency, including us. DM GROW for a short growth review."
On-screen: KHAAMAR BAARI SUPERMARKET | 400% REVENUE GROWTH | ONE YEAR | DM GROW
Uses the exact approved sentence. Add no other figures. Say "four hundred percent" in audio and confirm the pronunciation. The presenter is the Zenix CEO, never a stand-in for the client.

## 5. Production method (recommended)

**Winner: real voice, image-to-video lip-sync on your stills, real supermarket b-roll.**

1. **Record audio once.** The CEO reads all approved scripts in one 30-minute session with the same mic. Consistent, real audio removes the biggest AI tell. If a voice clone is used instead, get the CEO's written consent.
2. **Animate the stills.** Use image-to-video with audio-driven lip-sync from setups A, B, C, D. Generate short segments (5 to 8s) rather than one long clip. Assumption: this costs fewer credits per usable second and makes retries cheaper. Confirm the live price in the Higgsfield panel before locking the batch. Do not spend the 200 credits until Batch 1's five scripts and voice are locked.
3. **Add real footage.** Original content is what Instagram is prioritizing in 2026. Where possible, use phone footage from real supermarket shelves, produce, and store fronts (with permission), not generated grocery scenes. Khaamar Baari footage is the ideal source.
4. **Finish in an editor.** All exact text, captions, logos, prices, claims, and mockups get added in post.
5. **Label the content.** Meta requires its AI disclosure tool for organic posts with photorealistic AI video or realistic AI audio, and may penalize skipping it. Turn it on for every AI-presenter reel. The EU AI Act deepfake disclosure rules also start 2 Aug 2026 (relevant if you post to EU viewers; UK is not covered by it, check separately).
6. **Mix in real.** Alongside the 20 AI reels, film 1 to 2 real reels per week on a phone. Risk to test: an all-AI feed may hurt trust and reach.

Set up DM keyword automation to send the asset and ask two qualifying questions (business type, monthly marketing budget). Route qualified replies to a human.

## 7. Measurement and decisions

Primary metric: **qualified keyword requests** and **booked GROW calls**. Views, shares, and watch time are diagnostics.

Track per reel: 3-second hold, average watch time, sends, keyword DMs, qualified DMs, calls booked.

Proposed decision rules (these are starting thresholds, not industry benchmarks, and you have no baseline yet):
- After Batch 1, compare each reel to your own account's recent average.
- Keep a pillar if it produces at least 1 qualified keyword request in its first 2 reels. Double supermarket reels if they beat broad on qualified requests even with fewer views.
- Drop or rewrite a hook that has below-average hold and zero requests.
- If AI-presenter reels underperform your real phone reels on both reach and requests, shift the ratio toward real.
