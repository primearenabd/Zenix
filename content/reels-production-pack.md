# Zenix Reels: Production Pack (Batch 1)

For each video: topic, hook, full script, then every clip with its first frame and Seedance 2.0 prompt for Higgsfield. Status: draft for CEO and owner approval.

Recommended order: PAGE, GROW, MAP, STORE, PLAN.

---

## Shared blocks

### Voice block (Male Archie, same voice in every clip)
Paste at the end of every clip prompt.

> His voice is a calm, warm, confident adult male voice, clear natural English, steady pace, dry deadpan delivery, the same voice in every clip. His lips match every spoken line exactly. Only his voice, no music.

### Style block
Paste at the end of every clip prompt.

> Warm cinematic interior lighting, shallow depth of field, photorealistic skin and natural hand movement, 24fps. No subtitles, no logos, no watermark. No readable text anywhere except what is in the first-frame image.

### First-frame image block
Paste at the end of every first-frame image prompt. Use the matching studio reference image in edit mode. Do not generate a face from text alone.

> Vertical 9:16, 1080x1920, photorealistic. The man must match the reference image exactly: same face, hair, beard, watch. Keep the studio, lighting and outfit from the reference. Same camera distance, eye-level, warm cinematic lighting, shallow depth of field, natural skin texture. No text, no logos, no watermark, no extra fingers, no extra people.

### Studio references
- Couch: brown sweater, podcast mic, wood-slat wall (GROW)
- Office: navy blazer, marble table, green folder (PAGE, STORE)
- Armchair: cream sweater, green armchair, chalkboard (MAP, PLAN)

### Before generating
1. Generate the first frames, check identity, hands, props and studio match.
2. Run each reel's clip 1 first. Keep credits for one retry per reel.
3. Timings assume 2.6 to 2.8 spoken words per second. Adjust if the voice reads faster or slower.
4. Add all captions, labels, mockups and the 400% graphic in the edit. Never rely on generated text.
5. The CEO approves scripts, hook actions and the use of his likeness. Turn on Instagram's AI disclosure when posting.
6. Weekly cap and an owner for each review offer (PAGE, GROW, STORE, MAP, PLAN) before posting.

## Fallbacks
- If the face is blocked or a prop looks wrong, drop the prop and keep only the gesture; build the prop moment as an edited insert.
- If @ tags are not recognised, replace `@Image1` with "the first uploaded image".
- If the voice drifts between clips, record one clean voiceover of the full script and replace the audio in the edit.
- If speech sounds rushed, shorten the lines in that segment or extend the clip by 1 to 2 seconds.
