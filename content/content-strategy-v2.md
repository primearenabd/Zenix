# Zenix Reels: Better Content, Rails and Free-Tool Slate

What the research says, what was weak in the first ten reels, four repeatable series, twelve new concepts and four finished scripts.
Status: draft for owner review. Nothing is generated or published.

---

## What the research says

Third-party sources only. Check the two policy points on Meta's own pages before you rely on them.

- **What Instagram ranks:** watch time, sends (shares in DMs) and likes. Sends count most for reaching people who do not follow you yet. Sources: [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-algorithms/), [Later](https://later.com/blog/how-instagram-algorithm-works/), [Socialync](https://www.socialync.io/blog/adam-mosseri-shares-instagram-algorithm-2026).
- **Hooks:** the first 2 to 3 seconds decide it, and some sources say the first 1.7 seconds. About half of viewers watch on mute, so the hook needs a visual and on-screen words, not only speech. Looping the ending adds watch time. Sources: [Hootsuite](https://blog.hootsuite.com/instagram-reels/), [TechJuice](https://www.techjuice.pk/instagram-adam-mosseri-algorithm-ranking-creator-tips/).
- **What people save and send:** educational content, tutorials and checklists that solve one specific problem. Source: [SocialPilot](https://www.socialpilot.co/blog/instagram-reels-for-business).
- **Comment-to-get-the-link is allowed** when it runs through Meta's official API. The person must start it by commenting, the message must go out inside Meta's 24-hour window, and rate limits apply. Sources: [InstantDM](https://instantdm.com/blog/is-instagram-automation-allowed), [CreatorFlow](https://creatorflow.so/blog/instagram-dm-compliance-meta-rules/).
- **New AI label rule (announced 31 August 2026):** profiles that feature an AI-generated person must carry an "AI-generated profile" label, or their reach can be limited. Reports say people who only use AI tools as part of real work are not covered, and fully synthetic personas are. It is unclear where an AI version of a real, named CEO falls. Sources: [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2246914/instagram-will-demote-ai-generated-influencers-if-they-dont-clearly-label-their-account/), [Metricool](https://metricool.com/instagrams-new-ai-label/), [CreatorFlow](https://creatorflow.so/blog/instagram-ai-generated-profile-label/).

**What this changes:** an all-AI presenter feed is a reach risk. Reels that show something real (a screen, a result, a real clip) are safer, more believable and easier to send to a friend.

## What was weak in the first ten reels

- Most were good advice you can find anywhere. Nothing to see, try or keep.
- Almost every reel depended on the AI presenter talking for 30 seconds. Few had a "wait, that is free?" moment.
- The call to action asked for a review from us. A free tool or a ready guide is a stronger reason to comment.
- Few were written to be sent to someone ("send this to your manager").

## The fix: four rails

| Rail | Format | Why it gets saved or sent | Real footage needed |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Free Tool Friday** | One free tool, one result, three steps, screen recording | Useful, specific, repeatable, and people screenshot the steps | Yes: screen recording |
| **Makeover** | Before and after of a poster or post, with the three fixes | Visual proof, easy to send to a store owner | Yes: design screen recording |
| **Hot take** | One contrarian line and one fix (the best of the first ten) | Quotable, sparks comments | Presenter |
| **Proof and behind the scenes** | The approved Khaamar Baari result, how Zenix works, live automation demo | Trust, and shows the AI automation service working | Real footage where possible |

**Comment rail for every reel:** "Comment KEYWORD and Zenix will send you the guide" (or "the tutorial" or "the link"). Each keyword needs a finished asset and a comment-to-DM setup before the reel goes live.

## Four finished scripts

Each runs about 30 seconds. The presenter (real or AI) opens and closes. The middle is voiceover over a real screen recording. The steps below must be checked against the live screen before you record, since tool menus change.

### 1. ADS (Free Tool Friday)

**Hook (on screen and spoken):** "Every ad your competitor is running is public. And you can see them free."
**Opening visual:** He points at his phone. On-screen words: "SEE COMPETITOR ADS. FREE."

**Script:**
"Every ad your competitor is running is public, and you can see them free. Open Meta's Ad Library. Pick your country. Type the name of the store down the road. Now look: which offers are they pushing, and how long has each ad been running? An ad that keeps running is probably working. Copy the idea, not the ad. Comment ADS and Zenix will send you the guide."

**Screen recording beats:** open the Ad Library page, choose country, type a store name, scroll the results, highlight the "started running" date. Use a public business you are allowed to show, or blur the name.
**Guide to send:** one-page checklist: what to note (offer, creative, call to action, how long it has run).
**Why it could be sent:** "send this to your marketing person."

### 2. TRENDS (Free Tool Friday)

**Hook:** "Free tool: see what shoppers in your state are searching for right now."
**Opening visual:** He taps a phone showing a rising line. On-screen words: "WHAT ARE SHOPPERS SEARCHING?"

**Script:**
"Want to know what shoppers near you are searching for? Google Trends is free. Type a product, like mangoes or turkey. Choose your state. Then look at the last twelve months. See the spike? That is your promo window. A good starting point is to begin your content two weeks before it. Screenshot this, then comment TRENDS and Zenix will send you the guide."

**Screen recording beats:** open Google Trends, type a product, set the location to a state, set the range to 12 months, point at the peak.
**Guide to send:** a one-page calendar template and the exact steps.

### 3. MAKEOVER (Makeover rail)

**Hook:** "This poster has twenty offers. Watch me fix it."
**Opening visual:** a crowded sample poster fills the frame, then a clean version wipes over it.

**Script:**
"This poster has twenty offers, and nobody knows what to buy. Step one: pick the hero deal and make it huge. Step two: keep two supporting offers. Step three: move the store name and hours to the bottom. Delete the rest. Same store, same deals, and now you can read it at a glance. Comment MAKEOVER and Zenix will send you the template."

**Screen recording beats:** a fictional store poster in Canva or similar, before and after, each step highlighted. Keep all text exact and made in the design tool, never generated by the video model.
**Guide to send:** the poster template and the three-step checklist.

### 4. AUTO (Proof rail, live demo)

**Hook:** "Comment DEMO and watch your phone."
**Opening visual:** he points at the camera, then at his phone. On-screen words: "COMMENT DEMO".

**Script:**
"Comment DEMO and look at your phone. That message you just got? I did not send it. Here is how it works. One: put a keyword in your caption. Two: an automation tool watches the comments. Three: it sends your guide straight to their inbox. You can set this up for your own store. Comment AUTO and Zenix will send you the setup guide."

**Screen recording beats:** a test account comments DEMO, the DM arrives; then the automation tool's trigger and message screens (hide any names and keys).
**Compliance:** use a tool that works through Meta's official API, and only message people who commented.
**Guide to send:** the setup guide and a blank reply template.

## Seven proven formats to add

Found in the reference reel you sent (a creator breaking down why ranking videos go viral, about 97 seconds) and in your list. I sampled frames from it, about one every 8 seconds, so this is what the picture shows, not a transcript of the audio.

**What the reference does:**
- **Split screen:** a visual on top (a tier list with real view counts beside each example) and the presenter in a small window at the bottom.
- **Big word-by-word captions** in the middle, so it works on mute.
- **A three-point framework on screen** ("Progressive reveal, Controversial score, Commentary") that the speaker walks through.
- **Proof first:** the examples are real videos with their view counts shown.

**What we take from it:** the presenter does not have to carry every second. A small presenter window plus a visual built in the editor is cheaper, more reliable and more believable than generating B-roll with the video model. The presenter clips become simple continuous shots.

| Format | How it works | Zenix example | Keyword |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tier list (S to F)** | Items drop into a tier graphic one by one; one placement is a little controversial | Six supermarket post types ranked S to F | TIER |
| **Comparison** | Two versions side by side, viewer picks | Poster A (twenty offers) vs poster B (one hero deal) | POSTER |
| **Curiosity list** | Numbered list with a teased last item | Three things I check first in any supermarket's marketing | CHECK |
| **Common mistakes** | Named mistakes plus a five-minute fix | Three Google profile mistakes that can send shoppers away | MAP |
| **Listicle** | "Five things to do or not miss" | The seven-day content plan (WEEK) | GROW |
| **Q&A** | Start from a real question, answer it in 30 seconds | "Should I boost my posts?" | ASK |
| **Reaction** | React to a flyer, post or ad | A flyer a store owner sent us, with permission | REACT |

**Two integrity rules for these formats:**
- **Curiosity hook with the 400%:** "My client got 400% in a year by following these three tricks" says more than the approved claim allows. The approved sentence only says Khaamar Baari achieved 400% revenue growth in one year while working with Zenix. Do not present three tips as the cause. A safe version keeps them apart: state the approved sentence, then say "here are three things I check first in any supermarket".
- **Q&A and reaction need real material.** Use real questions and flyers that people sent you, with permission, and anonymise them. Never invent viewer comments or submissions, and do not pick on a named competitor.

## Format scripts (about 30 seconds each)

Layout for all of them: top half is the graphic, bottom is the presenter window, with big word-by-word captions in the middle. Tier placements and "winners" are our opinion and should be said that way.

### 5. TIER (tier list)

**Hook:** "I ranked six supermarket post types from S tier to F tier."
**Graphic:** a blank tier list. Each post type drops in as it is spoken (progressive reveal).

**Script:**
"I ranked six supermarket post types, S tier to F tier. F tier: the buy now graphic. Nothing to learn, nothing to save. D tier: a blurry flyer photo. C tier: a price list. B tier: a staff introduction. A tier: a recipe using this week's deal. S tier: answering a real customer question in the store. Opinions, not rules. Comment TIER and Zenix will send you the guide."

**Guide to send:** a one-page post-type cheat sheet. Invite disagreement: "What would you move?" in the caption.

### 6. COMPARE (poster A vs B)

**Hook:** "Same store. Same deals. Two posters. Which one wins?"
**Graphic:** poster A and poster B side by side, built in a design tool with exact text.

**Script:**
"Same store, same deals, two posters. Poster A has twenty offers. Poster B has one hero deal and two supporting ones. Look at both for two seconds. Which one can you remember? That is the test, and I would bet on B. Comment A or B. Then comment POSTER and Zenix will send you the template."

### 7. CHECK (curiosity list)

**Hook:** "Three things I check first in any supermarket's marketing. Number three is the one everyone skips."
**Graphic:** a three-line list; the third line stays blurred until the reveal.

**Script:**
"Three things I check first in any supermarket's marketing. Number three is the one everyone skips. One: what is this week's offer, in one sentence? Two: can a shopper find your hours on Google in ten seconds? Three: what happens after someone messages you? If you have no answer to three, start there. Comment CHECK and Zenix will send you the guide."

### 8. MISTAKES (common mistakes)

**Hook:** "Three Google profile mistakes that can send shoppers to your competitor."
**Graphic:** a real screen recording of a business profile, with each mistake highlighted.

**Script:**
"Three Google profile mistakes that can send shoppers to your competitor. One: holiday hours that were never updated. Two: the wrong category, so you show up for the wrong searches. Three: photos from years ago. Fix all three in five minutes. Comment MAP and Zenix will send you the guide."

### 9. ASK (Q&A template)

**Hook:** "A store owner asked me: should I boost my posts?"
**Graphic:** the real question on screen, anonymised.

**Script:**
"A store owner asked me: should I boost my posts? Short answer: sometimes. Boost a post that already gets saves and comments, with one clear offer and one action. Do not boost a post nobody reacted to. Boosting makes a post louder, not better. Got a question like this? Comment ASK and we will answer yours."

**Note:** this keyword collects questions for the next Q&A reel. Replace the sample question with a real one before filming.

### 10. REACT (reaction template)

**Hook:** "A store owner sent me this flyer. Here is my honest reaction."
**Graphic:** the real flyer with permission, blurred where needed.

**Script structure:** first reaction (what works, one line), the biggest problem (one line), one fix (one line), then "Comment REACT and send us yours." Write it after you have a real flyer.

## Production approach

1. **Real screen recordings carry the middle of every tool reel.** They are free to produce, accurate, and real content.
2. **The presenter opens and closes.** Use real clips of the CEO where you can. Use the AI presenter for the rest only after checking the AI-label rule below.
3. **One voice across the reel.** Record a single voiceover of the script (the CEO, or one TTS voice) and use it over both the presenter and the recordings.
4. **Words on screen in the hook,** since many people watch muted. Loop the ending back to the hook line.
5. **Captions and graphics** are added in the edit.

## Before you post

- Confirm on Meta's own pages whether the AI-generated profile label applies to an AI version of the CEO. If in doubt, label it.
- Every keyword needs a finished guide and a working comment-to-DM reply, using Meta's API.
- Do not name or show a real competitor's private data. Public ad pages are fine, but blur or choose examples carefully.
- Free-plan limits change. Check each tool's current free limits before you call it free.
- The CEO and owner approve everything before it is published.
