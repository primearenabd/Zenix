## Comparison videos

### Ads

#### C1. Boost a post or run a proper ad?

*Which should a supermarket use. Decision rule.*

**Script**

> Boost a post or run a proper ad: which should a supermarket use? Boost is quick. You pick a post, a budget and an audience, and go. A proper ad gives you more control over the goal and who sees it, but it takes longer to set up. If you are new and have one clear offer, start with a boost. If you want more control, move to a proper ad. Either way, fix the offer first. Comment BOOST and Zenix will send you the guide.

#### C2. Google Ads or Meta Ads?

*Which should a supermarket use. Decision rule.*

**Script**

> Google Ads or Meta Ads for a supermarket? Google shows your ad to people already searching, like grocery store near me. Meta shows your offer to people scrolling, so it suits this week's deals. If people search for what you sell, try Google. If you want to show a deal, try Meta. Start with one, and measure store visits, calls or messages. Comment CHOOSE and Zenix will send you the guide.

#### C3. Organic posts or paid ads?

*Where should a new supermarket start. Decision rule.*

**Script**

> Organic posts or paid ads: where should a new supermarket start? Organic is free, but it is slow if few people follow you. Ads reach people nearby faster, but they need a clear offer, or you pay to be ignored. If you have no followers and one strong offer, try a small local ad. If your offer is unclear, fix that first with organic posts. Comment START and Zenix will send you the guide.
