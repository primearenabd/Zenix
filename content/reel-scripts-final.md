# Zenix Reels: Final Scripts (Batch 1)

Status: draft for CEO and owner approval. Each script is about 30 seconds (61 to 67 spoken words). Recommended production order: PAGE, GROW, STORE, MAP, PLAN.

---

## 1. PAGE

**HOOK:** "You wouldn't hide the milk in the basement. Your landing page does."
**HOOK VISUAL:** He picks up a plain, unbranded milk carton from the side table on "milk". On "basement" he lowers it out of frame, below the table. On "Your landing page does" he looks straight into camera with one raised eyebrow. Edit: small thud when it drops.
**CTA keyword:** PAGE

**Full spoken script:**
"You wouldn't hide the milk in the basement. Your landing page does. Someone clicked your ad for one offer, and now they're hunting through banners to find it. If the deal isn't obvious, they leave. Put your ad's exact offer at the top. Add the price, one reason to trust you, and one clear button. Comment PAGE and Zenix will send you the guide."

---

## 2. GROW

**HOOK:** "Nobody follows a megaphone."
**HOOK VISUAL:** He lifts a toy megaphone toward his mouth, stops, and says nothing. He lowers it and sets it aside, then delivers the line, deadpan. The megaphone makes no sound.
**CTA keyword:** GROW

**Full spoken script:**
"Nobody follows a megaphone. If your supermarket's page only shouts buy now, why would anyone stay? Three posts, same pitch, nothing to answer, nothing to save, nothing to share. Answer one real customer question in every sales post. Show how a product is used, or let your team speak. Then connect that post to a sale. Comment GROW and Zenix will send you the guide."

---

## 3. STORE

**HOOK:** "Four hundred percent. That's not a typo."
**HOOK VISUAL:** He leans in slightly with one hand flat on the table. On "not a typo" he taps the table once and points to the side, where the number sits. Edit: a "400%" count-up (0 to 400%) built in an editor, one tick sound as it lands. The number is on screen from the first frame.
**CTA keyword:** STORE

**Full spoken script:**
"Four hundred percent. That's not a typo. Khaamar Baari Supermarket achieved four hundred percent revenue growth in one year while working with Zenix. Specific proof builds trust, and a specific offer does too. Lead with your strongest deal. Show the price and where to find the store, in one short video. Then make sure your Google listing matches. Comment STORE and Zenix will send you the guide."

Proof note: the approved sentence is spoken unchanged. Add no other figures. The tips are general advice, not a claim about what was done for the client.

---

## 4. MAP

**HOOK:** "Your store sign says open. Google says closed. Guess who shoppers believe."
**HOOK VISUAL:** He holds a phone beside his face and taps it twice on "Google says closed". On "Guess who shoppers believe" he lifts his eyebrows and tilts his head at the phone. Edit: cut to storefront b-roll with a lit open sign on "store sign". The "Closed" listing is a generic mockup, never a real store or the real Google interface.
**CTA keyword:** MAP

**Full spoken script:**
"Your store sign says open. Google says closed. Guess who shoppers believe. Some won't call to check. They'll just go elsewhere. Nearby shoppers can be gone before they ever reach your door. Check today's hours, then weekends and holidays. Test your phone number, directions, and photos on a customer's phone. Comment MAP and Zenix will send you the guide."

---

## 5. PLAN

**HOOK:** "The Boost button doesn't make a post better. Just louder."
**HOOK VISUAL:** He shakes his head slowly on "better". On "louder" he taps a glowing button on his phone. Edit: a volume swell sound effect rising on "louder". The BOOST label is added in editing.
**CTA keyword:** PLAN

**Full spoken script:**
"The Boost button doesn't make a post better. Just louder. So before you press it, answer one thing: what should a stranger do next? Visit, call, or message? If you can't name it, you're paying to show a vague post to more strangers. Pick one offer, one audience, one action. Put the offer first. Comment PLAN and Zenix will send you the guide."

---

## Approvals and checks before generation

1. The CEO approves each script, each hook action, and the use of his likeness and voice.
2. Timed read-through with the real voice. Over 30 seconds: cut the last sentence before the CTA.
3. A finished guide and a comment reply ready for each keyword (PAGE, GROW, STORE, MAP, PLAN).
4. All on-screen text, labels, mockups, and the "400%" graphic are added in editing, never generated.
5. Turn on Instagram's AI disclosure when posting.
6. Confirm Higgsfield accepts the CEO reference image before spending credits.
