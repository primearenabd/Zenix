## Approach

1. **Split-screen layout.** Top half is a graphic or a screen recording built in the editor. Bottom is the presenter in a window. Big word-by-word captions sit in the middle. This matches the format in the reference reel you sent.
2. **The presenter clips are simple.** Each clip is one continuous speech across three hard cuts (medium, close-up, medium), with no B-roll and no props. That is easier for the video model and cheaper to retry. The interest comes from the top half.
3. **Bookend reels (ADS, TRENDS):** the presenter opens and closes, and a real screen recording plays in the middle with voiceover.
4. **Every clip is generated on its own,** with its own first-frame image, the video prompt that starts from that image without re-describing it, and the same @Charon voice line.
5. **Comment rail:** every reel ends with "Comment KEYWORD and Zenix will send you the guide". Each keyword needs a finished guide and a working comment-to-DM reply first.
6. **Sound:** keep one voice per reel. For bookend reels, record or generate the middle voiceover in the same voice as the clips, or replace all audio with one track.

**Set up once:** upload and tag the studio images `@presenter-couch`, `@presenter-office` and `@presenter-armchair`. Paste the first-frame image prompt, generate the image, then upload it as the start frame and paste the video prompt.
